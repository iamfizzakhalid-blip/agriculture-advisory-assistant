In recent years several new races have been identified. New races are known to arise through hybridization, mutation and introduction from neighbouring countries. However, the barberries do not play any part in the annual recurrence of the rust in India, but they may be instrumental in creating new races by hybridization. Then it becomes necessary to eradicate the barberry bushes from pockets of infection in the hills. Comparison of the Symptoms of the Three Wheat Rusts Black rust (Puccinia graminis tritici) attacks the stem most severely, then the leaf sheaths, leaf lamina and ear. Its uredosori are large, elongated, running together and bursting early, forming large fragments of the epidermis in doing so; colour dark brown, becoming darker gradually as teleutospores are formed in the same sori (pustules), found on all green parts of the plant. Its telia or teleutosori are much elongated and black in colour, bursting rather early, found on all green parts of the plant, but least on the leaf blades. Yellow rust (P. striiformis) attacks the leaf lamina most severely, than the leaf sheaths, stem and ear.
