Agricultural Stress and Vegetation Conditions FAO's Agricultural Stress Index (ASI) tracks the percentage of cropland affected by drought conditions across Pakistan each year, based on the Vegetation Health Index (VHI). At the national level, this indicator has varied widely from year to year, ranging from below 1 percent of cropland affected in some years, such as 1994 and 2000, to over 30 percent in drought-affected years such as 1989, 1991, and 2001. In the most recent years for which data is available, national cropland stress was estimated at about 1 percent in 2024, 8.6 percent in 2023, 4.2 percent in 2022, 17.8 percent in 2021, and 6.2 percent in 2025. Provincial breakdowns are also available, generally showing higher and more variable stress levels in Balochistan and the northwestern regions compared with Punjab and Sindh, which host the bulk of wheat production. Other FAO/GIEWS Data Sources Referenced Additional detail on wheat supply, utilization, and trade balances for Pakistan is available through the FAO/GIEWS Country Cereal Balance Sheet (CCBS). Price time series are available through the FAO/GIEWS Food Price Monitoring and Analysis (FPMA) Tool. Seasonal, vegetation, and precipitation maps, along with downloadable time-series data on the Agricultural Stress Index, NDVI, Vegetation Health Index, and precipitation, are available through FAO/GIEWS Earth Observation for Crop Monitoring. Food security phase classifications are available through the Integrated Food Security Phase Classification (IPC) initiative. Broader long-run statistics on Pakistan's wheat area, production, and yield are available through FAOSTAT and the FAO Country Profiles page for Pakistan.
