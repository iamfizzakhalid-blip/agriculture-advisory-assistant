CROP GROWTH STAGES Understanding wheat growth stages helps time irrigation, fertilizer, weed control, and disease/pest scouting correctly: 1. Germination and emergence: Seed absorbs moisture, seminal roots and the first leaf (coleoptile) emerge. 2. Seedling establishment: Early leaf development, crown (adventitious) root system begins forming. 3. Tillering: The plant produces multiple side shoots (tillers) from the crown; this stage largely determines the number of ears (spikes) the plant will eventually produce. 4. Stem elongation/jointing: Internodes elongate, the stem grows upward, and nodes become visible; the growing point rises above the soil surface, after which frost damage becomes more serious. 5. Booting: The flag leaf (last leaf) emerges and the developing ear swells within the flag leaf sheath. 6. Heading/ear emergence: The ear emerges fully from the flag leaf sheath. 7. Flowering (anthesis): Pollination occurs; this is a highly sensitive stage to heat stress, frost, and moisture stress. 8.
