What is the structure of the wheat market and storage? About 65 to 75 percent of the total wheat produced in Pakistan is stored at the farm. Smaller farms generally keep more grain for consumption, so the quantity of wheat entering commercial channels from farms of up to 4.5 ha is insignificant.
