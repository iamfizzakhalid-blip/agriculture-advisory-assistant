The exact rate depends on the sowing method, seed purity, germination percentage, and the variety being sown. Sowing Method Wheat can be sown by broadcasting, or through the Kera and Pora methods, as well as by using an automatic Rabi drill. Fertilizer For rainfed wheat, the recommended fertilizer dose is 85:58:58 kg per hectare of NPK. For irrigated wheat, the recommended dose is 115:65:65 kg per hectare of NPK. Half of the nitrogen is applied along with the full amount of phosphorus and potassium at sowing, and the remaining half of the nitrogen is applied with the first irrigation. In sandy soils, nitrogen is applied in three splits: at sowing, and with the first and second irrigations. Irrigation Wheat generally requires 4–6 irrigations, totaling about 19 acre-inches of water. Irrigation is important at the tillering, booting, earing, milky, and grain formation stages. Plant Protection Measures Plant protection in wheat covers the management of weeds, insect pests, and diseases.
