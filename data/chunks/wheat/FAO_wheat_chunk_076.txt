When there is insufficient grain to fill a bag, the bag can be cut and sealed at any length. • Natural control of insect pests. • Natural control of fungus and toxin synthesis. • Trade speculation possible at very low cost. • Maximized profitability of permanent storage facilities, enabling increased storage capacity according to seasonal needs. • Traders able to develop new markets or regions rapidly and with very low investments. Disadvantages • Large exposed surface; • climatic risk, mainly from hail storms; and • rodents – mainly mice – which use loose or unstretched parts of the bag for housing.
