Unless the benefits from reduced wheat losses and handling costs are transferred to the elevator owner/ operator, such a project will not be financially viable. Incomplete utilization of an elevator’s capacity after construction – because of the transition to bulk handling, changes in local government policy, or competition with traditional wheat storage and handling systems – poses an additional major risk for investors. Sensitivity to risks As with any other investment project, the feasibility of grain elevators in Pakistan depends on a number of key variables and risks, such as possible increases in investment and operating costs, or a sudden decrease in revenues.
