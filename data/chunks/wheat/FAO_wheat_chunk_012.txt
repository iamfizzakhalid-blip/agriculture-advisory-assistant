Wheat, lentils, tobacco, rapeseed, barley and mustard are Rabi crops; rice, sugar cane, cotton and maize are Kharif crops (FAO, 2004). The marketing year (MY) for wheat in Pakistan runs from May to the following April – for example, MY 2011/12 starts on 1 May 2011 and ends on 30 April 2012. Most wheat is produced on irrigated land, so crop performance is closely linked to the supply of irrigation water and farm inputs, such as quality seeds and fertilizers.
