Normally the wheat crop takes 120-140 days to get fully mature. The crop is cultivated in the start of November up to the mid of December. In order to investigate the chief causes about variation in the crop’s growth, development and yield related to varying weather conditions and some other factors during each phenological stages of wheat crop, different meteorological parameters are studied as well.
