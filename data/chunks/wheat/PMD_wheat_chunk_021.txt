Crop coefficient (Kc) is actually the ratio of maximum crop evapotranspiration to reference crop evapotranspiration. For wheat, this ratio becomes 1 during the reproductive cycle (heading to grain formation) otherwise it remains less than 1 bearing minimum values during the early age of the crop and at maturity. The crop water requirement was calculated for the period from emergence to wax maturity. After wax ripeness practically, there is no need of irrigation because the hot and dry conditions are desirable to achieve rapid hard maturity.
