The water erodibility of soil on sloping ground is governed by a number of factors including: < the percentage of vegetative cover on the soil surface; < the amount and intensity of rainfall; < the soil infiltration rate and permeability of water through the soil; < the slope and the nature of the underlying subsoil strata and bedrock. The loss of organic matter and soil structure as a result of overcultivation can also give rise to significant soil loss by wind erosion of exposed ground.
