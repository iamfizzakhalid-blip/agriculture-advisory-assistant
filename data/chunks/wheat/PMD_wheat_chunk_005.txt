Agriculture is the mainstay of Pakistan's economy. It accounts for 21% of the GDP and together with agro-based products fetches 80% of the country’s total export earnings. More than 43.7% of the labor force is engaged in this sector. [4] The crops are most vibrant sub- sector of agriculture, it consists of 39.6% of agriculture and 8.3% of GDP.
