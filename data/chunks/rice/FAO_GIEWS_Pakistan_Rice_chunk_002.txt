Trade History Rice, particularly Basmati and non-Basmati varieties, is Pakistan's largest cereal export. Reported export figures include: 3.3 million tonnes in 2014; 3.5 to 3.8 million tonnes in 2015; 4.4 million tonnes in 2016 (7 percent above 2015, supported by competitively priced non-Basmati supplies); around 3.9 to 4.2 million tonnes in 2017; 4.1 million tonnes in 2018 (up 12 to 14 percent, reflecting the 2017 record crop); 3.9 million tonnes in 2019; around 4.0 to 4.8 million tonnes in 2020 (estimates varied between an initial forecast and a later, lower figure reflecting reduced supply); 4.6 million tonnes in 2021 (up 17 percent from 2020, reflecting adequate availabilities from the bumper 2020 output); an above-average level forecast for 2023, supported by ample exportable surplus and increased buyer interest following India's imposition of export restrictions on non-Basmati white and broken rice; a record 6.5 million tonnes reported for 2024
