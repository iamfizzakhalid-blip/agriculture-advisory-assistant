INTEGRATED PLANT NUTRITION MANAGEMENT (IPNM) A fertile soil provides a sound basis for flexible food production systems that, within the constraints of soil and climate, can grow a wide range of crops to meet changing needs. Integrated plant nutrition management is an indispensable tool for supporting high crop production systems through soil fertility improvement while giving due consideration to ecological concerns. The basic principle underlying the concept of IPNM is the maintenance and improvement of soil fertility through integration and optimization of all possible plant nutrient resources (i.e. organic, inorganic and biological) appropriate to individual farming situations in their ecological, social and economic perspective, for increased crop productivity and quality. For developing IPNM practices, the cropping systems rather than an individual crop, and the farming systems rather than the individual field, are the focus of attention.
