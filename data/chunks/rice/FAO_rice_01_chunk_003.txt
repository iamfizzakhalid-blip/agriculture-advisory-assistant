Weed control: Keep rice field weed free up to maximum tillering stage Initial plowing buries weeds, allow germinate their seeds and decomposed by later plowings. Stubble cleaning after every laddering can control weeds. Use weed-free seed and keep tools and machinery clean Keep levees and irrigation canals, non-farm areas free of weeds Weedicides are very effective in weed control: o Grasses/broadleaf weeds: spray Stam F34 (Propanil) @ 9-11 lit/ha at 3-4 leaf stage. o Broad leaf grasses: spray Rice Spray 70 (MCPA) @ 2-3 lit/ha at 5-6 weeks after sowing o Grasses, broadleaf & sedges: Spray Basagran PL 2 (bentazon + propanil) @ 8-10 lit/ha at 3-4 leaf stage Avoid rainfall up to 4-8 hours after spraying. Again, Field should be completely drained before application of chemicals and flooded 3 days after spraying Mechanical control of weeds by operating finger weeder at 15-20 days after sowing combined with one hand weeding was more economical.
