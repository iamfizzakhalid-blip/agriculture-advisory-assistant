There are three groups of weeds found in rice fields: weeds of the grass family, weeds of the sedge family, and broad leaf weeds. Common Rice Weeds Weeds of the grass family: Swanky grass (Echinochloa colona) Dhedan (Echinochloa crusgalli) Khabal grass (Cynodon dactylon) Narro (Paspalum distichum) Broad leaf weeds: Mirch boti (Sphenoclea zeylanica) Chopti (Marsilea minuta) Darai boti (Eclipta prostrata) Weeds of the sedge family: Ghoein (Cyperus difformis) Bhoein (Cyperus iria) Deela (Cyperus rotundus) Insect Pests Major insect pests of rice include the Pink Rice Borer and Yellow Stem Borer (both stem borers), and the Leaf Hopper, which causes the whole field to appear white when infestation is heavy. Diseases Common rice diseases include Root Rot, Collar Rot, and Stem Rot. Rice Blast (locally known as Jhulsao) can cause up to 80% yield reduction. Harvesting Early maturing varieties are harvested 90 days after transplanting, while late maturing varieties are harvested 140 days after transplanting. Harvesting time is indicated when the panicle turns golden. Coarse varieties mature earlier than fine varieties.
