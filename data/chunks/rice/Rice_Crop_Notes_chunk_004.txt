Seed priming: Seeds are primed using CaClI and KCl salt solutions, a practice used by farmers in Sialkot and Faisalabad districts. Nursery seedlings raised this way are healthy and vigorous, and are ready for transplantation within 25–30 days. Seedlings from primed nurseries show better growth, yield better kernel quality at harvest, and show greater disease resistance. Direct Sowing Direct sowing involves irrigation, seed bed preparation at the proper moisture condition, and drilling of soaked or treated seeds. Transplanting Transplanting involves flooding (heavy irrigation), puddling, and then transplanting the seedlings. Sowing Time Fine varieties: 1st June to 1st July Coarse varieties: 20th May to 1st June Transplanting is done at 25–30 days of nursery age Direct sowing is done earlier than transplanting Hill distance: 25 cm Direct sowing is generally carried out around the time of nursery sowing, or about two weeks after the stated sowing time. A "hill" refers to the row-to-row and plant-to-plant distance point where seedlings are transplanted. Two seedlings are placed per hill for a 25–30 day old nursery, and four seedlings per hill for a nursery older than 40 days. Fertilizer Coarse varieties: 200:75:60 kg/ha (NPK) Fine varieties: 125:60:60 kg/ha (NPK) Irrigation Rice requires 15–16 irrigations, with standing water maintained throughout and no dry period. Weedicides are also used as part of water management. Weeds Weed management in rice relies on standing water, along with cultural and chemical control methods. Weed Management Weeds can cause 15–20% yield losses, and sometimes up to 50%.
