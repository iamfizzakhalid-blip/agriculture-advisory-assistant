Rice — Crop Production Notes Introduction and Local Names The scientific name of rice is Oryza sativa L., and it belongs to the family Poaceae. In Urdu, rice is called Dhan or Chaval, and in Punjabi it is known as Munji. Crop Characteristics Rice is an annual crop with hypogeal germination. It is a short-day, Kharif-season crop with a determinate growth habit and is self-pollinated. Its seed is referred to as grain. Origin Rice is believed to have wild forms in Asia, Africa, and America. Oryza sativa (Asian rice) contains two major subspecies: the sticky, short-grained japonica (or sinica) variety, and the nonsticky, long-grained indica variety. Oryza glaberrima is the African species. Rice cultivation is believed to have begun about 5,000 years ago in China, and it reached the USA in the 17th century. Top Rice Producing Countries According to FAO (2013) data, the leading rice-producing countries and their production figures (in tonnes) were as follows: China produced 203,612,192 tonnes, India produced 159,200,000 tonnes, Indonesia produced 71,279,712 tonnes, Bangladesh produced 51,500,000 tonnes, and Vietnam produced 44,040,457 tonnes. Rice Production in Pakistan (2014) In 2014, the area under rice cultivation in Pakistan was 2,890,646 hectares, production was 7,002,832 tonnes, and the yield was 2.42 tonnes per hectare. In 2014, Pakistan ranked 13th in world rice production. Province-wise Contribution (2010–13) Of the total varieties grown, 25% were hybrid and 75% were conventional varieties. By area under cultivation: Punjab accounted for 71% (1.8 million hectares), Sindh 22% (0.75 million hectares), Baluchistan 5% (0.18 million hectares), and KPK 2% (0.06 million hectares). By production: Punjab accounted for 63% (3.4 million tonnes), Sindh 31% (2.8 million tonnes), Baluchistan 4% (0.6 million tonnes), and KPK 2% (0.1 million tonnes).
