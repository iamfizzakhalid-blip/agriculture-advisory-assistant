Rice production in South America during the last two decades (with the exception of the last 2 years) has seen a rapid decline in the area planted with rice, accompanied by a steady increase in overall production. Yield advancement permits production to continue increasing, despite the removal of large areas from rice cultivation. Although increases in national yield are the norm for most South American countries, not all countries are advancing at the same rate. Yield improvement in Bolivia was insignificant during the last 25 years, as a result of the concentration of production in the unstable upland sector.
