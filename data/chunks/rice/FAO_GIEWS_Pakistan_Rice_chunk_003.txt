Pakistan is generally recognized among the world's top rice-exporting countries, having been described in various years as the world's fifth-largest and, more recently, closer to the world's tenth-largest rice producer overall. Agricultural Stress and Vegetation Conditions FAO's Agricultural Stress Index (ASI) tracks the percentage of cropland affected by drought conditions across Pakistan each year, based on the Vegetation Health Index (VHI). This is a national and provincial indicator covering all cropland, not specific to rice alone, but it provides useful background on seasonal growing conditions during the paddy season. At the national level, cropland stress was estimated at about 1 percent in 2024, 8.6 percent in 2023, 4.2 percent in 2022, 17.8 percent in 2021, and 6.2 percent in 2025. Provincial data shows that Sindh and Punjab, the country's main rice-growing provinces, generally experience lower average stress levels than Balochistan and the northwestern regions, though flooding rather than drought has been the more frequent cause of rice crop losses in recent years, particularly in 2022 and 2025.
