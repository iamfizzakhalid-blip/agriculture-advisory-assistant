At the time of development of the ear, brown stains appear on the leaf blade, leaf sheath, and also on the upper internodes of the culm as the ears come out of the leaves. The symptoms become quite prominent when the ears come out of the leaf sheath. There are two types of Ufra disease symptoms: (1) Thor, or swollen ufra, and (2) Pucca, or ripe ufra. In the case of thor, the ear remains enclosed within the leaf sheath and forms a spindle-like sheath, whereas in pucca (the ripe ufra), the ear comes out of the leaf sheath. In swollen ufra, there is no grain formation, whereas in ripe ufra the grains are formed at the tip of the peduncle, and other flowers remain unfertilized. Nematodes The nematodes appear in abundance as cottony masses in different parts of the plant. The nematodes perennate in the soil from November to June, till the appearance of the new crop. When humid conditions prevail, the nematodes climb up the stem and infect the growing parts of the plants. Reproduction takes place on the rice plants. The eggs measure 60 to 80 by 16 to 20 microns. The larvae are produced from the egg, and ultimately fully grown nematodes are developed. The nematodes are supposed to be obligate parasites. Sanitation: crop residues should be destroyed by burning, which lowers the nematode population in the soil. Proper drainage and avoidance of water logging is recommended. Seed treatment: seeds should be treated with a suitable nematocide before sowing.
