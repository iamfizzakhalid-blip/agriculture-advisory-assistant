In the soft dough stage, the starch in the grain is beginning to become firm but is still soft. In the hard dough stage, which includes the end of grain filling (R7) and the grain-drying stage (R8), the whole grain is firm and almost ready for harvest. Maturity At maturity, the whole grain is hard and ready for harvest. This stage is reached at approximately 20 to 22 percent grain moisture.
