Cleaned and treated seed can prevent seed-borne diseases and ensure healthy seedling and good harvest. Seed sorting: can be done in 3 ways (1) Winnowing: using a Kula or air current/fan (2) By hand: if small amount (3) By soaking in urea solution Dissolve 1.5 kg urea in 40 liters of water Soak seeds in the urea solution & stir to float unfilled, broken & diseased seeds Remove floating poor seeds by hands/sieves Wash seeds properly 3-4 times with clean water. Clean seed will have bright appearance with no spots & lesions. Seed rate, Soaking and Incubation technique Seed Rate: A. Broadcasting: About 60-70 kg ha-1 seed is needed B.
