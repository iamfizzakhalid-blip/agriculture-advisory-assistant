In that case herbicide application should be done in the evening after sowing. DSR crop does not require continuous submergence and can be safely irrigated when hairline cracks appear on the soil. Moisture stress should be avoided at tillering, panicle initiation, and grain filling stages which are crucial for obtaining higher yields. At these stages it is advisable to keep fields flooded. The last irrigation should be applied 20 days before harvesting. DSR is an aerobic rice production and may show iron deficiency symptoms in some soils. Flooding or submerging the field corrects these symptoms. Major weeds of DSR: In Pakistan, the major DSR weeds are bermuda grass (Cynodon dactylon L.), climbing dayflower, jungle rice, purple nut sedge, egyptian grass, bulrushrice, small flower umbrella plant, flat sedge and most importantly horse-purslane (Trianthema portulacastrum L.). An integrated approach of controlling weeds in DSR can be adopted as sometimes single use of herbicides has been found to be unable to control a broad spectrum of weeds.
