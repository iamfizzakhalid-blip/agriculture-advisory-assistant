Symbolically the total cost can be written as: TC = FC + VC - (1) Where, TC = Total Cost FC = Fixed Cost VC = Variable Cost Fixed Cost (FC): The cost, which is incurred even if no output is produced, is referred to as fixed cost. The fixed cost is also known as sunk cost. Variable Cost (VC): The variable cost is the cost that can vary with the change in output. Labor, fertilizer and insecticides etc. are the variable cost items. Marginal Cost (MC): Marginal cost is the change in TC resulting from a unit increase in output.
