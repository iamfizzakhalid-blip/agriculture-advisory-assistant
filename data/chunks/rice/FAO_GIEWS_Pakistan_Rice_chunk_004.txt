Other FAO/GIEWS Data Sources Referenced Additional detail on rice (paddy) supply, utilization, and trade balances for Pakistan is available through the FAO/GIEWS Country Cereal Balance Sheet (CCBS). Price time series are available through the FAO/GIEWS Food Price Monitoring and Analysis (FPMA) Tool. Seasonal, vegetation, and precipitation maps, along with downloadable time-series data on the Agricultural Stress Index, NDVI, Vegetation Health Index, and precipitation, are available through FAO/GIEWS Earth Observation for Crop Monitoring. Food security phase classifications are available through the Integrated Food Security Phase Classification (IPC) initiative. Broader long-run statistics on Pakistan's rice area, production, and yield are available through FAOSTAT and the FAO Country Profiles page for Pakistan, which also lists an active FAO technical cooperation project on sustainable and regenerative management of rice production in Pakistan.
