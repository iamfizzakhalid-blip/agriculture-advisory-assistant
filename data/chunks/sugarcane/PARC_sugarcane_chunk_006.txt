RESULTS AND DISCUSSION Short-tailed mole rat, Nesokia indica, inflicts significant damage to sugarcane crop; however, the damage inflicted to different varieties of sugarcane by the specie is unknown. The feeding trial experiment was carried out to know the difference in the grams sugarcane consumed for the four commercial varieties of sugarcane, namely, Gulabi- 95, Triton, BL-4 and Thatta- 10 by the specie. The suga rcane and rice consumption by each rat were recorded for five days. As per results (Table-1), BL-4 was found the most preferred variety. The variety may be preferred due to its softness and high sucrose contents.
