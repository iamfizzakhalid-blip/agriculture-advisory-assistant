A soil test before planting, and periodically during a multi-year ratoon cycle, is recommended, since sugarcane's long duration and high nutrient offtake make it particularly important to track soil fertility trends over time rather than relying on a single blanket recommendation for the life of the crop. WEED MANAGEMENT Because sugarcane grows relatively slowly in its early establishment and tillering phases before forming a dense canopy, the crop is vulnerable to weed competition particularly during roughly the first three to four months after planting (or after ratoon initiation), after which the closing canopy naturally suppresses much subsequent weed growth by shading.
