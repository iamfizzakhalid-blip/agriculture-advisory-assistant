The mean data showed a maximum cane yield of 150.84 t ha-1 for YtTh-1705, followed by YtTh-1707 (145.42 t ha-1) and YtTh-1701 (142.29 t ha-1). Whereas, the minimum yield of 104 t ha-1 was recorded in YtTh-1730 against the check variety (137.09 t ha-1). Similarly, the maximum CCS of 12.08% was displayed by YtTh-1705, followed by check variety Thatta-10 with CCS of 11.73%. Whereas, the CCS% in other sugarcane genotypes was low as compared to check variety. Among the inspected sugarcane genotypes YtTh-1705 was found promising due to highly improved performance for quantitative and qualitative parameters. Therefore, can be included in the gene pool for further varietal development studies.
