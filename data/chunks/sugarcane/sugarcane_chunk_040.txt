Agents, Dealers and Contractors There are dealers who offer the farmers a trouble free passage for their stock and a prompt payment. These contractors buy the stock from the farmer near the farm give the grower on the spot cash payment and take the stock to the factory on their own transport. It is proved that most agents, who offer the farmers a trouble free passage for their stock and a prompt payment, are private individuals in no way linked to the mills.
