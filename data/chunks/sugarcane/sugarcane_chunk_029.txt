Pricing and Quality In a report by the CCP, it is explained how the Governments, both at the Federal and Provincial level, are key players in the balance of the sugar industry and its market. The provincial governments fix a ‘support price’ for sugar; this price is the bare minimum that should to be paid to the farmer per 40 kg of sugarcane. This ‘support price’ is based on the ‘indicative price’ set by the Federal Government this indicative price is not a definite number but a price range. This range according to MINFA is based on a number of factors which the provincial government takes into account while putting in the ‘support price’. For instance the cost of production of sugarcane, average wholesale prices of sugar, prices received by cane growers in the last year, import parity based on average FOB London price of white sugar, export parity based on average FOB London price of white sugar and an average of all the figures taken into consideration.
