The spirit of working for a society needs to be awakened. The term ‘sustainability’ is fearsome to the corporate world as well as growers as it is usually related to reduced productivity or profit. However this is not the case measures reducing environmental impacts will more than often also provide economic benefits for farmers and mills. Awareness of this approach will provide an opening to reconcile environmental and social needs with the long-term development of the sugar industry.
