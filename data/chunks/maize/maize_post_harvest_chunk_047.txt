A weak nutritional aspect of maize is the quality of its protein since around a half of its protein is made up of zein, which is low in two essential amino acids, lysine and tryptophan. Fortunately this deficiency nowadays has been corrected with the development of the quality protein maize (QPM), which is nutritionally the most superior cereal grain. 1.6.2.
