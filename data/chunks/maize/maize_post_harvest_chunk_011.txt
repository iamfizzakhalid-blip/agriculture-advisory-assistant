Some special advantages of the maize beside the broad global distribution, its lower price, the diverse type of grains and the biological and industrial properties which make the maize an adequate product for its utilisation. The maize has a very wide range of uses than any other cereal. It can be used as staple food for human consumption, animal feed and for many industrial uses. The highest rate per caput supply of maize occurs in countries where most of the grain is for feeding animals or where the maize represents the preferred staple food, as is shown in table 4.
