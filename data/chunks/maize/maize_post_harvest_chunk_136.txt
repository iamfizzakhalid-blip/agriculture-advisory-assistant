Grease your 9-inch skillet with about 2 tablespoon of shortening or oil (Use bacon drippings instead if available). Leave oil in bottom of pan... Place pan in oven to heat... Combine cornmeal, 2 eggs, buttermilk, and the melted bacon drippings. Mix well. A whisk does this nicely. Pour into hot skillet. Batter will sizzle. Bake at 450 degrees for 35 minutes or until golden brown. Serves 6 to 8. Hint: Sprinkle a little cornmeal in the hot pan before adding the batter. It will brown and add a crispier texture. If using plain cornmeal, add 2 teaspoons of baking powder and 1/2 teaspoon of salt for each cup of regular meal.
