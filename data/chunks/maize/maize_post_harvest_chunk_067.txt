The use of pack-animals (donkey, mules, oxen’s) is of the most profitable and convenient method, in small scale transport. This method is suitable on plain surface but also feasible in hilly and mountainous areas. Donkeys are ideal as pack-animals since they are tolerant, patient and require minimum supervision and control. Normally, for transporting some padding must be used over the back of the beast to offer comfort. A donkey averaging 100-110 kg of weight can carry load of 25-50 over distances up to 20 km. Large donkeys with proper care can carry load 50-75 percent of their own weight over short distances. In average, daily loads of 40-100 kgs (35 percent of body weight) for up to 4 working hours are considered normal, see figure 17.
