It is difficult to assess or quantify because is a subjective criteria, but can be described by comparison with quality standards · Nutritional loss: reduction of the food value of the crop. It can be qualitative and quantitative loss and is also difficult to identify · Germinative loss: represent a reduction in germination ability. Methods of laboratory exists to measure the degree of viability of a sample of seeds · Economic loss: reduction in monetary value of the product due a reduction in quality and quantity. Since quality loss is difficult to quantify it is also difficult to quantify economic loss. 3.1.3. factors and causes of grain loss.
