Disease Incidence and Yield Losses In Pakistan, the average yield loss is 5 to 15 per cent, with severe cases reaching up to 20 per cent. Globally, common yield losses range from 5 to 20 per cent, with epidemics causing losses of 25 to 30 per cent. In Pakistan, the disease is common in the maize-growing areas of Punjab and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (KPK). The usual incidence is 10 to 25 per cent, and it can reach up to 40 per cent in wet seasons. Worldwide, the disease is found in the USA, India, China, Brazil, and Africa, with disease incidence ranging from 15 to 60 per cent depending on climate. Management Strategies Integrated disease management for Brown Spot of maize includes the use of resistant hybrids, crop rotation, removal of crop debris, improved drainage, balanced fertilization, avoiding dense planting, and the use of fungicide only if the disease is severe. Conclusion Brown Spot is a significant disease of maize, strongly linked to wet environmental conditions. Early detection is critical, and integrated management ensures protection of yield.
