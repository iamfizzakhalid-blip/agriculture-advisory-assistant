Traditional insect pest control methods. Traditional methods used for farmers to reduce infestation of the crop may fall into three groups: 1). Special practices 2). Use of material such as ashes (for its abrasive and letal effect on the insects cuticle), mineral and oil in which physical barrier effects are responsible for the control of insects and 3). The use of whole or parts of the plants where there may be some natural insecticidal, fungicidal or repellent effect (mainly alcohols, alkaloids and terpenes).
