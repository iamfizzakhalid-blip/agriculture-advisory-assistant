Consumption marketing: The end-uses of maize at the farm level can be for sale, seed or food owners’ family and feed for livestock. *Depending if the cob is shelled immediately of later. Advantages of the Timely Harvesting System: · Avert long-term exposure of maize crop in the field. It reduces damage and losses due to birds, insect pests, rodent and wild animals. · Reduction of damage and losses means better returns to the farmers in terms of quality and quantity crop. · Less pest field infestation means less problems in storage · Less risks of crops theft in the field · Ensure early ploughing and planting of the next crop. This ensures also higher crop yield, better crop rotation and better future harvest. · Ensure that the plant biomass (leaves, stalks, etc) still have significant levels of nutrients for feeding livestock · The abundant plant biomass collected under this system can also be used for cooking, heating of water and spaces for lighting in rural areas. · Heavy to handle and transport the bulky high-moisture
