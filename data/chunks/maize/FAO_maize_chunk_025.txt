Year round Maize Dwarf Mosaic Causal organism: Maize dwarf mosaic virus (MDMV) or Sugarcane Mosaic Virus (SCMV), and Maize Chlorotic Mottle Virus (MCMV) Vector: Rhopalosiphum maidis (Corn leaf aphid) - The symptom starts with the chlorotic spots on the base of young leaflet. These spots later develop short chlorotic streaks parallel to the veins. - The infected leaf develops a distinct mosaic having the mottled area of pale green and dark green colours, and yellow spots. - If the infection occurs during seedling stage, there may be severe stunting of the plant. As plants approaching maturity, the foliage can turn purple or reddish purple. - The combination of MDMV and MCMV infections is characterized by mosaic appearance, leaf blight, and bud necrosis. - Tassels become malformed or barren.
