Seed Rate and Seed Treatment The recommended seed rate for maize is 8–10 kg per acre. For seed treatment, Imidacloprid 70WS is applied at 7 grams per kg of seed, and Topsin-M 70WP is applied at 2 grams per kg of seed. Sowing Method Maize can be sown by broadcasting for fodder purposes, or using an automatic Kharif drill or a dibbler/maize planter, at a depth of 3–4 cm. Row-to-row spacing is 60–75 cm, and in spring planting, ridges are oriented east to west, with sowing done on the south side of the ridges. Plant-to-plant spacing is 15–20 cm, giving a plant population of about 30,000 plants per acre. Fertilizer (For Soils of Medium Fertility) For hybrid maize, the recommended fertilizer rate is 110-46-25 kg per acre of NPK. At sowing, 2 bags of DAP and 1 bag of potassium sulphate are applied; this is followed by 1 bag of urea at each of the 0.75 ft, 2 ft, and 3 ft growth stages, and a further 1 bag of urea before flowering. For synthetic varieties, the recommended fertilizer rate is 95-35-25 kg per acre of NPK. At sowing, 1.5 bags of DAP and 1 bag of potassium sulphate are applied; this is followed by 0.5 bag of urea at the 0.75 ft stage, and 1 bag of urea at each of the 2 ft and 3 ft stages, as well as before flowering. Irrigation The first irrigation is given 3–4 days after germination. Subsequent irrigations are applied at 7–10 day intervals in light soils and 10–15 day intervals in heavy soils.
