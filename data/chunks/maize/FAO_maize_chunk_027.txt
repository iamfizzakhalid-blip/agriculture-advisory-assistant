Corn Thrips Scientific name: Frankliniclla williamsi Hood, Caliothrips sp. and Thrips hawaiiensis (Morgan) Adult thrips are black with two pairs of wings. They are often found in leaf-sheath axils and inflorescences, suckling the sap from them. The infested leaf shows the pale yellow mottling and later becomes wilt and dies eventually. The outbreak occurs in every stage of maize planting. - Inspection of young leaf bases, leaf-sheath axils, and inflorescences. Corn Leaf Aphids Scientific name: Rhopalosiphum maidis (Fitch) Aphid larvae and adults feed by sucking sap from leaves, plumules, and tassels, causing curly leaf. If the infestation is severe, the tassels will become wilt and weak, and will not develop to flowering stage.
