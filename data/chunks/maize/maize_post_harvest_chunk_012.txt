(Source: FAOSTAT, 2003) The maize as human food consumed directly in quantities higher than half of its production is found in Andean countries of South America, Mexico, Central America and the Caribbean, Africa and South and Southeast Asia. The maize grain used for human consumption in these regions use mainly varieties of white maize rather than yellow maize.
