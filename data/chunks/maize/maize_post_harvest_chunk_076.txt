During this period, the level of infestation of the cob by pest is insignificant, therefore is not necessary chemical treatment for the cob before or they are drying in the structure. · After cob are dried, they must be removed for shelling, cleaning, and pre-storage treatment to protect the grain from damage by insect pest. Grain is best stored in shelled form, in bulk or bagged. · Rodent pest are controlled by rat-guard structures (See figure 22 below) which are set up 0.9 m from the ground around the vertical holding pole of the crib.
