If the severe infestation is Asian Corn Borer Scientific name: Ostrinia furnacalis (Guenee) Adults are moths with a wingspan of approximately 3 cm. Anterior pair wings are copper colour with transverse wavy lines at the edges and the bases. Two tiny black spots are at the middle of each anterior wing. Posterior pair wings have lighter colour than anterior pair wings and almost have no spot or pattern. Eggs are deposited in clusters overlap like fish scales. Larvae have creamy pink bodies with spots on each body segment. Mature larvae are approximately 20 mm long. Larvae begin boring into the top leaves, tassels, and stalk, and destroy in every stage of the plant growth. Asian corn borers cause the most damage when they attack during the tassel formation stage. The insect outbreak is found in all planting areas. - Normally, the population of Asian Corn Borer is controlled by natural enemies such as Trichogramma spp. and earwigs which will feed eggs and larvae of Asian Corn Borer and result in the prevention of an outbreak.
