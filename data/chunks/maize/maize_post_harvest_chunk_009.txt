The differences on yield are due mainly to environmental, technological, economic and organizational factors. In most developed countries the climate is temperate; likewise they use sufficient inputs and a well mechanised system for the maize production. As complementary information, the average production in tons per hectares for industrialised countries is 7.9, in contrast in developing countries is only 2.5.
