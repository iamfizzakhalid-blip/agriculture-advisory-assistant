Corn (maize) production for 2026/27 has been forecast at about 9.6 million tonnes, supported by better availability of irrigation water and continued momentum in planted area and yields, with modest losses expected from autumn-season monsoon flooding offset by promising prospects for the spring crop. Trade History Maize exports from Pakistan have generally remained modest compared with rice, though they rose sharply in years of exceptionally large harvests. Reported export figures include a well-above-average level of 1.4 million tonnes in the 2023/24 marketing year, attributed to the above-average 2023 harvest and lower domestic poultry feed demand resulting in high exportable surpluses. In most other recent marketing years, including 2024/25 and 2025/26, maize exports have been forecast at an average level of about 500,000 tonnes. Agricultural Stress and Vegetation Conditions FAO's Agricultural Stress Index (ASI) tracks the percentage of cropland affected by drought conditions across Pakistan each year, based on the Vegetation Health Index (VHI). This is a national and provincial indicator covering all cropland, not specific to maize alone, but it offers useful background on seasonal growing conditions during the maize planting and growing periods.
