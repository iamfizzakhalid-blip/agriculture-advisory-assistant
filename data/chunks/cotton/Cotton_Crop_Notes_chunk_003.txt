The boll contains locules, typically 4, 5, or 6 in number. Cotton Picking Once the petals dry and the boll is completely opened, the cotton is ready for harvesting or picking. Primary Tillage Primary tillage involves 4–5 ploughings to break the hard pan, followed by 2–3 plankings and land levelling, typically using a mould board plough. Seed Bed Preparation Seed bed preparation involves 2 ploughings and 2 plankings to produce a very fine seed bed. Moisture should be slightly more than optimum, but not so wet that it hinders cultivation. Sowing Time General sowing period: mid-April to end of June American cotton: 15 May – 15 June Desi cotton: April Seed Rate Desi cotton: 12–15 kg/ha American cotton: 20–25 kg/ha Plant population: 70,000–75,000 plants/ha Sowing Method Cotton is sown using line sowing methods such as the Kharif drill or ridge sowing. Row-to-row spacing is 60–75 cm, and plant-to-plant spacing is 15–38 cm. Sowing is best done in the morning or evening, at a depth of 5–6 cm. Seed Treatment Seed treatment methods include chemical treatment and soaking. Fertilizer The recommended fertilizer dose is 100:70:70 kg/ha of NPK (Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium). One-third of the nitrogen is applied along with the full amount of phosphorus and potassium at sowing. The remaining nitrogen is split, with one-third applied at the first irrigation and the final one-third applied at flowering. Irrigation The first irrigation is delayed until 40–45 days after sowing. This delay allows the root system to develop, since early irrigation would prevent roots from going deeper into the soil; it also restricts weed emergence. In total, 5–6 irrigations are given at intervals of 10–15 days.
