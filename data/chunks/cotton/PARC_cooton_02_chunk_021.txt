Cotton leaf curl virus is a crucial disease of cotton and its presence is increasing day by day in Pakistan due to the varying and supportive climatic conditions. The present studies showed peak incidence of ClCuV in the 1st week of September with maximum temperature ranging from 33-40°C, minimum temperature 25- 30°C and high humidity 52-80%. The correlation results showed a significant and positive relationship with relative humidity and negative association with maximum and minimum temperature. Hameed et al.
