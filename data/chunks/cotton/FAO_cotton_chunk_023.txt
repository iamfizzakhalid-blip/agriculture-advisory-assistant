These decisions are usually economic decisions about pest control - if I don't spray, will I lose some yield that is worth more than the cost of the spray? The decision requires knowledge of the ecosystem: recognition of pests and natural enemies, understanding of the interaction of pests and natural enemies, cotton plant and its ability to compensate. The decision also requires knowledge of the effect of pests on yields and the effect of pesticides on natural enemies. We have seen that sampling is the first step in making decisions.
