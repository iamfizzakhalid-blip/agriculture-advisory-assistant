Surviving bollworms will overwinter in the leftover bolls attached with cotton sticks and plant debris which re-infest cotton crop in the following season. Populations of bollworms are also controlled with mating disruption, chemicals, and releases of sterile males which mate with the females but fail to fertilize their eggs. Despite all out efforts made during off season, this pest can play havoc with the cotton crop. Hence it is mandatory to have a close check on its population during the cotton season.
