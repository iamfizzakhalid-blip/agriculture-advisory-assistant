Disease groups In order to be able to discover about disease management, one should appreciate information that is already available on life cycles of diseases. This exercise taps the information known by members of the group and links it up to practical field school situations. The exercise should not/does not “test” participants’ knowledge on diseases but summarizes the knowledge available and triggers creative thinking about how to find out about and manage diseases.
