Parasitism Measuring the parasitism level of caterpillars in cotton (This exercise can be done several times during the season.) Certain larval growth stages act as host for some parasites. The best way to learn about larval parasites is to observe what emerges from larvae collected from the field. Observe parasitoids of caterpillars (bollworms or leafworms) Explain the importance of parasitism Explain aspects of the biology of parasitoid species, e.g., are they restricted to certain larval growth stages on the host 30 plastic tubes with labels Fresh plant material for larval feeding 1. This exercise could be conducted at any time that caterpillars are common in the field. 2.
