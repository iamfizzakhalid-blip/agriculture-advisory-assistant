Beyond fiber, cottonseed is a valuable by-product, processed into cooking oil and high-protein animal feed (cottonseed cake/meal), adding significant additional value to the crop beyond lint alone. In many cotton-growing regions, the crop is a major source of rural income and employment, both in cultivation and in associated processing and manufacturing industries, and it is also a significant export commodity for a number of producing countries. Cotton stalks and other residues remaining after harvest can additionally be used as fuel or incorporated into soil as organic matter, though residue management is also important for reducing the carryover of certain pests and diseases into the following season.
