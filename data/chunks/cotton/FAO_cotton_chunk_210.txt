Soils Water infiltration rates The water content of the soil is as much an important biological indicator of the soil condition as its soil respiration. Soil respiration allows microorganisms to perform their function of breaking down organic material that the plant can use for its development. Knowing more about the type of soil a farmer has in his field has will help him make decisions about how he can improve it for his benefit.
