Crop rotation helps reduce the buildup of soil-borne diseases such as Fusarium and Verticillium wilts, which can persist in soil for extended periods under continuous cotton cultivation, and helps manage certain soil-dwelling pests and weed populations associated with continuous cotton monocropping. Rotating with legume crops can also help improve soil nitrogen status ahead of the following crop. In many systems, cotton is grown as the main kharif (summer) crop, with the field then prepared for a rabi (winter) crop such as wheat after the cotton picking season concludes, requiring careful timing of the final cotton pickings and field clearance to allow timely sowing of the following crop. MECHANIZATION IN COTTON PRODUCTION Cotton production can involve mechanization at several stages, though the degree varies considerably by region and farm scale: tractor-drawn implements for land preparation (plowing, harrowing, ridging); precision planters for accurate seed placement, spacing, and depth, particularly important given cotton's sensitivity to plant population; sprayers for herbicide, fungicide, and insecticide application; and, in some large-scale production systems, mechanical spindle pickers or strippers for harvest, often used in conjunction with chemical defoliation to promote uniform boll opening suited to machine picking. In many smallholder and labor-abundant production regions, however, hand picking remains the dominant harvest method due to its ability to selectively pick only mature bolls and produce cleaner lint, despite being more labor-intensive than mechanical picking. ECONOMIC AND INDUSTRIAL IMPORTANCE Cotton is one of the most economically important non-food agricultural commodities globally, serving as the primary natural fiber for the textile industry and supporting extensive value chains from farming through ginning, spinning, weaving, and garment manufacturing. Beyond fiber, cottonseed is a valuable by-product, processed into cooking oil and high-protein animal feed (cottonseed cake/meal), adding significant additional value to the crop beyond lint alone.
