Deep plowing, especially important for cotton given its deep taproot system, to break any hardpan and allow roots to penetrate deeply for moisture and nutrient access. 2. Subsequent harrowing/cultivation to break clods and produce a fine, workable seedbed. 3. Leveling the field to ensure uniform irrigation water distribution and to avoid waterlogging in low spots, which cotton is particularly sensitive to. 4. Incorporating farmyard manure or compost before final land preparation to improve soil structure, organic matter, and moisture-holding capacity, particularly valuable in medium to heavy soils prone to compaction. 5. Removing residues of the previous crop and controlling perennial weeds before sowing. 6. In regions using ridge-and-furrow or bed planting systems, forming ridges/beds at this stage to improve drainage and facilitate irrigation management through the season. COTTON VARIETIES AND SEED SELECTION Choice of variety significantly affects yield, fiber quality, pest/disease resistance, and management requirements. Key factors in variety selection include: - Maturity duration (early, medium, or late maturing), which affects the length of the growing season needed and suitability for the local frost-free window and any subsequent crop in rotation. - Yield potential under local management, irrigation, and soil conditions. - Fiber quality traits relevant to market value, including staple length, fiber strength, fineness (micronaire), and uniformity. - Resistance or tolerance to major pests, particularly bollworms (where Bt varieties are used) and sucking pests such as whitefly and jassids. - Resistance or tolerance to major diseases prevalent in the region, such as cotton leaf curl virus in areas where it is endemic, and bacterial blight. - Growth habit and plant architecture (compact vs. spreading), which affects plant population recommendations and suitability for mechanical operations. - Response to irrigation and fertilizer inputs. Farmers should select varieties recommended by local agricultural research
