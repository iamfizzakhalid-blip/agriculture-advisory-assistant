Insect Zoo Insect Zoos is done by participants in the training to help them learn about insects and natural enemies by direct observation and manipulation. Insects and spiders are more interesting when seen alive and active. A living organism is much more than what is seen in an alcohol filled jar. In fact some things can only be recognized when living. The activity and behavior of insects and natural enemies can only be seen in live specimens. The Insect Zoo will give you many living specimens for demonstration that will keep farmers more involved and help them remember better that predators and parasites are friends in the field.
