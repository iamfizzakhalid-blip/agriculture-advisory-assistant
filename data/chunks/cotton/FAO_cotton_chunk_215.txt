Describe how nutrients from the soil move through the plant Explain the role of water in nutrient uptake from the soil Water, red ink or dye, two cups per group, plants and two straws 1. Go outside by group and find many kinds of plants including rice seedling, rau muong, celery, 2. grasses and other plants. 3. Add water to the two cups and place several drops of the red food coloring. The water should be dark red. 4. Place the plants in the cups with the stems in the cups. Also place the straws in the cups. One straw should be flattened first. Place the plants in a bright place. 5. Wait 90 minutes and observe the plants. What happened to the color of the leaves? How has the red coloring moved in the plants? What does this imply about nutrient uptake from the soil? 6. How do plants take nutrients from the soil?
