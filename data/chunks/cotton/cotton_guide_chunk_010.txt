A soil test before the season is recommended, since nutrient requirements vary with soil fertility, texture, and cropping history; over-application of nitrogen in particular is a common cause of yield and quality problems in cotton due to its strong effect on vegetative-versus-reproductive growth balance. WEED MANAGEMENT Cotton is a relatively poor early-season competitor with weeds due to its slower initial growth compared to many weed species, making early weed control particularly important. Common weeds vary by region but often include various grasses and broadleaf species.
