Ask each small group to recall their experiences on seeing dead or infected insects in the field. Each group should then draw their observations on big paper for presentation. After all groups have presented their outputs, introduce the specimens of virus-infected insects from the laboratory. Ask each group to describe symptoms of the specimens from the laboratory. (Note: Insects infected by viruses become weak and activity is slowed down; the body color is changed; the cuticle becomes fragile and ruptures easily when touched, releasing the body content which has become liquefied. Dead larvae may be found hanging from or lying on leaf or plant surfaces with no filamentous structure on the cuticle.) 1.
