Many farmers spray NPV without seeing immediate kill of the target insect. This is because NPV acts more slowly than conventional chemical insecticides, thought it is no less effective. Before actual death occurs, feeding by larvae stops. This slow action often causes farmers to think that NPV is not effective. However, the benefits of NPV (conservation of parasitoids and predators, overall minimal health risk to farmers and consumers, minimal adverse effects on the environment) far outweighs the benefit of speedy killing of caterpillars using chemical insecticides. Moreover, resistance (in the target caterpillars) to chemical insecticides has rendered insecticides less effective than NPV.
