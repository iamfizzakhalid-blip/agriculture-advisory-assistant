(Thank you to ideas and field studies from Dr. James Mangan.) Spraying Adapted from Helen Murphy’s Guide for Farmer-to-Farmer IPM Health Studies Spraying pesticides is dangerous. The compounds used for spraying are in a concentrated form which makes them even more dangerous than usual exposure. Concentrated liquids direct from the bottle, and exposure to sprays in the field during application causes numerous symptoms such as skin rashes, dizziness, nausea, and headaches. The usual recommendation for gloves, boots, rain clothes, and respirator are impossible to implement for most farmers because of the costs. While some farmers use “protective” clothing they do not fully understand how pesticides enter the body and how so-called “protective” clothing does NOT guarantee that contamination will not occur.
