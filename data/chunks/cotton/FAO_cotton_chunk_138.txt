Poison sprayer maintenance Pesticides are not medicines! They are poisons to be used and handled with great care. Some granular chemicals can be broadcast (using gloves and boots). But many compounds are liquid and need to be sprayed on the crop. Proper maintenance of the sprayer is necessary to avoid direct exposure from leaking valves, leaking hoses, bad nozzles, and bad rubber rings on the tank openings. Many sprayers spill chemicals on to the back of farmers as they are spraying. Proper maintenance is also required to have complete coverage of the palnt. A nozzle that is old or clogged will not give good coverage of the plant. Many poorly maintained sprayers put out a stream of poison like a person urinating. This wastes money, and exposes the farmer to heavy dripping of the poisons. The spray should be small droplets and spread over the entire spray path.
