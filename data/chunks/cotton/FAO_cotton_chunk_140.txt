General introduction and effect on diseases: Climate and weather are important for the growth and development of diseases, insects, natural enemies and plants. Climate is the long term general pattern of the daily weather patterns. For example, the climate of the South of Vietnam is hotter than the climate of the North of Vietnam. On a particular day however, the weather in the North may be hotter than in the South. The weather is difficult to predict, while climate is easier to forecast. In fact, weather scientists cannot reliably predict the weather for more than about 24 hours, even with super computers and vast information networks.
