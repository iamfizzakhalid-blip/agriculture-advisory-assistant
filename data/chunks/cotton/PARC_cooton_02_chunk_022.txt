Novelty Statement In current scenario of climate change and its impact on the abundance of different insect pests, it is high- ly important to have relevant data on the population fluctuation of different insect pests from time to time. This paper provide novel data on the population trend of different insect pests of cotton and CLCuV inci- dence with respect to environmental factors in Mu- zaffargarh district. This study would be helpful in timely initiating various control tactics for the man- agement of insect pests and to minimize yield losses.
