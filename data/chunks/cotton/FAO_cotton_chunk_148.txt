Demonstrate how weather parameters (temperature and moisture) are different over small areas of several meters. Big paper, pencil Thermometers (2 per group), plastic bags, scales 1. Choosing sites: choose two locations. The locations should be in the following places: a. place with no shade and high on a slope b. place with lots of shade and high on a slope c. place with no shade and low on a slope d. place with lots of shade and low on a slope 2. Soil moisture: at each site, place some soil in a plastic bag. One handful is enough. Close the bag. Weigh the content of the bag. Dry the soil in the sun. Weigh the soil with the bag again after drying. The difference in weight is the amount of water in the soil. The original soil weight was the weight of water plus the non-water portion. {Original % moisture = [(weight of water)/(original weight - water weight)] x 100}. 3. Temperature: use the thermometer to measure three positions for each site: a. soil temperature b. air temperature above a plant c. air temperature at the bottom of the plant 4. Make a table of temperatures and soil moisture for the sites. 5. Can you explain why there are differences in soil moisture at each site?
