Study of spread of fungal leaf spot Observe the spread of a fungal disease from an infected to a healthy plant Four healthy potted plants Leaf spot infected leaves Small hand sprayer Clear plastic bags Tissue First day preparations: Germinate spores by inserting the leaf spot infected leaves in a plastic bag with moist (not soaking wet) tissue paper. Close the bag tightly but leave some air inside to avoid rotting. Leave the bag overnight. Second day: Bring the potted plants into the classroom. Put clean tap water into the container of the small hand sprayer. Spray two potted plants with clean water. Label one plant ‘healthy control, uncovered’. Cover the other plant with a plastic bag and label the plant ‘healthy control, covered’. Prepare the disease inoculum by grinding and squeezing out extract from leaves and adding this to water OR by stirring the leaf portions with leaf spots in a glass with clean water. Transfer the inoculum to the small hand sprayer. Spray the inoculum on the other two potted plants.
