What could have caused this to happen? Sensitivity of Bt to sunlight This study will show how sunlight breaks Bt down. Since Bt is a biological agent, it is sensitive to sunlight. In bright sunlight, it loses its effectiveness and strength to kill caterpillars. After this study, you should be able to appreciate the effect of sunlight on the effectiveness of Bt and to make appropriate decisions on how to apply Bt.
