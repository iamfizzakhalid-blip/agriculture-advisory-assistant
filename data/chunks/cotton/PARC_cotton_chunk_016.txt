A study showed that 20 Bt cotton adjustment brings about critical low pesticide utilize, higher yield, higher gross margins, environmental benefits as far as higher farmland biodiversity and lower soil and groundwater tainting. The authors noted that these positive externalities are valued at US$79 per section land (US$195/hectare), which adds another 39% to the advantages as far as higher gross margins. Including monetary and outer advantages brings about aggregate advantages of US$284 per section of land (US$701/hectare), or US$1.7 billion for the whole Bt cotton area in Pakistan [28].
