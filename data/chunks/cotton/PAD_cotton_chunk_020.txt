DGA (Research) AARI, Faisalabad GOVERNMENT OF THE PUNJAB HOME DEPARTMENT Lahore the, 09th January, 2020 O R D E R WHEREAS, it has been reported that Pink Bollworm is the most threatening insect pest of cotton. Larvae of this pest hibernate in the left over bolls of the cotton. Left over bolls are the main source of hibernation of the pink bollworms larvae. Resultantly, such infested bolls become hatcheries for the insect pest which severely damage the cotton crop in the next season.
