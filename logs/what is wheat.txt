User:
What are the common diseases of wheat?

Assistant:
Common diseases of wheat are mainly the rusts:

- **Black (stem) rust** – caused by *Puccinia graminis tritici*
- **Brown/orange rust** – caused by *Puccinia recondita*
- **Yellow (stripe) rust** – caused by *Puccinia striiformis*

These rusts are the most important and destructive wheat diseases worldwide, often leading to significant yield losses.
