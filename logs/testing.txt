User:
Why can't I just save seed from my hybrid maize crop and replant it next year?

Assistant:
Hybrid maize seed does not breed true. If you save seed from a hybrid crop and replant it the next year, the second generation will not maintain the same yield, uniformity, or stress tolerance. Therefore farmers are advised to purchase fresh hybrid seed each season.

User:
How can I tell if my sugarcane is affected by red rot disease?

Assistant:
Red rot shows these clear signs:

- **Stalk interior** - split a stalk lengthwise; look for a reddish discoloration with white cross-bands.
- **Smell** - a sour odor from the infected stalk.
- **Leaves** - wilting, drying, drooping, and a reddish or yellow-red discoloration that can be mistaken for drought or nutrient stress.
- **Overall vigor** - reduced cane growth and yield.

If you see the reddish internal pattern and the sour smell, the crop is likely infected. Leaf symptoms alone are not enough to confirm red rot.
