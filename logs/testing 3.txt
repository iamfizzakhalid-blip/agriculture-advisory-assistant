Why do sugarcane leaves sometimes turn reddish, and could that be a disease or a nutrient issue?

Assistant:
Red-tinged leaves in sugarcane are usually a sign of **red rot disease** rather than a nutrient problem.
- Red rot shows reddish discoloration with white cross-bands inside the stalk and a sour smell.
- Surface leaf reddening can be confused with drought or nutrient stress, but true red rot is confirmed by splitting the stalk to see the internal reddening.

Nutrient deficiencies in sugarcane typically cause pale yellowish or interveinal yellowing, not a reddish hue.

Bt cotton aur regular cotton mein kya farq hai, aur yeh pest control ke liye kyun ahem hai?

Assistant:
- **Bt cotton** aik genetically modified variety hai jo Bt gene rakhta hai jo crystal (Cry) protein banata hai.  
- **Regular cotton** mein yeh gene nahi hota aur is liye Cry protein produce nahi hota.  

**Yeh pest control ke liye kyu important hai**

- Cry protein specific insect pests ke gut ke receptors se attach hota hai (jaise bollworms).  
- Yeh insect ke gut lining ko damage karta hai, paralysis aur maut ka sabab banta hai, jis se pest damage kam hota hai.  
- Pait ke andar pests ko maar kar Bt cotton se bahar se insecticide lagane ki zaroorat kam ho jati hai aur paidawaar (squares, flowers, bolls) intact rehte hain.
