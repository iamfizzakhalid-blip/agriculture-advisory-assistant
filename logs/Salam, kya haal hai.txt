Why do sugarcane leaves sometimes turn reddish, and could that be a disease or a nutrient issue?

Assistant:
Red-colored leaves in sugarcane are usually a sign of **red rot disease**.
- Red rot produces reddish discoloration on leaves and a sour smell; the stalk shows internal reddening with white cross-bands when split open.
- The reddish leaf symptom can be mistaken for drought stress or a nutrient problem, but typical nutrient deficiencies (nitrogen, potassium, micronutrients) cause yellowing or interveinal streaking, not a true reddish hue.

So, if leaves turn reddish, it is most likely a disease rather than a nutrient issue.
