User:
At which specific growth stage is irrigation considered most critical for wheat yield, and why does missing this irrigation cause such significant damage compared to missing others?

Assistant:
The most critical irrigation period for wheat is during the crown root initiation (early tillering) and the grain‑filling stages (milk to dough). Water stress at crown root initiation limits root development and grain number, while stress during grain filling reduces grain weight and final yield. Missing irrigation at these stages therefore causes a larger yield loss than missing it at other times.
