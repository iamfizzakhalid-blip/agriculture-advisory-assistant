how wheat is made

Assistant:
The process of making wheat flour involves several steps. First, the grain is cleaned to increase the moisture content, making it easier to separate the bran and germ from the endosperm. Then, the grain is milled using a roller milling process to get the best possible separation of endosperm from the bran. The flour extraction rate depends on the type and design of the mill. The milling process yields generally 72 to 74 percentage flour, with the rest being mill feed. In some countries, whole-grain wheat is milled to produce whole-wheat flour, with a recovery rate as high as 99 percentage.
