Which fertilizer should I use for maize?

Assistant:
For maize, it is recommended to use a combination of chemical and organic fertilizers. Chemical fertilizers provide essential nutrients such as nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium, while organic fertilizers improve soil structure, increase microbial activity, and add trace elements. A balanced NPK mix (e.g., 10-10-10 or 15-15-15) can be applied, and cover crops like mungbean or sunnhemp can be grown to further enhance soil fertility.

What are the common diseases of maize?

Assistant:
- Leaf blight (caused by *Helminthosporium turcicum*)
- Rust (Common Rust *Puccinia sorghi* and Southern Rust *Puccinia polysora*)
- Brown spot (caused by *Physoderma maydis* / *Physoderma zeae‑maydis*)
- Head smut (caused by *Sphacelotheca reiliana*)
